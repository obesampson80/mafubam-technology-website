Problem

Strong research can look weak when headings, tables, figures, references and page layout are inconsistent. The task is to make the document easier to read while respecting the owner's content and institution guidelines.

What MAFUBAM delivered

  • Improved heading structure and document hierarchy
  • Aligned tables, figures, captions and numbering
  • Prepared cleaner page layout for Word and PDF output
  • Checked visible consistency across sections
  • Kept author identity and source document content out of the public sample

Sample visual

Outcome

The finished document is easier to review, navigate and present because the formatting supports the content instead of distracting from it.
