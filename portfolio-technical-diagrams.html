Problem

Many research and implementation projects need diagrams that are more precise than ordinary sketches. The challenge is to show system parts, data flow and user interaction without making the report visually confusing.

What MAFUBAM delivered

  • Recreated rough system ideas as clean architecture visuals
  • Prepared workflow and process diagrams for report sections
  • Used consistent labels, spacing and hierarchy
  • Separated technical logic from decorative design
  • Prepared visuals that can sit cleanly inside Word, PDF or slides

Outcome

The resulting diagrams make the technical scope easier to inspect, explain and present during review or defence.
