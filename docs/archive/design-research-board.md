# Design Research Board

This board captures visual and UX directions to explore before producing mockups.

## 1. Design Position

MAFUBAM should sit between:

- A professional technology consultancy
- A research-support service
- A practical local business services company

It should not look like:

- A SaaS startup
- A crypto/AI landing page
- A school assignment service
- A generic web design agency

## 2. Visual Motifs To Explore

Useful motifs:

- Document grid and annotation marks
- Clean chart fragments
- Technical diagram lines
- Website wireframe snippets
- Folder/project workflow cues
- Subtle Port Harcourt/local business context

Avoid motifs:

- Floating code rain
- Abstract glowing spheres
- Robot/AI imagery
- Random server racks
- Anonymous people in suits shaking hands

## 3. Layout Concepts

Concept A: Proof-Led Professional

- Hero combines headline with a compact "workbench" preview.
- Preview shows document, chart and website snippets.
- Best when portfolio samples are ready.

Concept B: Two-Path Service Gateway

- Hero prioritizes Research Solutions and Business Technology Solutions as equal paths.
- Best when visitors arrive from mixed referrals.

Concept C: Founder-Led Local Expert

- Hero includes founder/company identity, Port Harcourt location and practical service promise.
- Best when founder photo and story are available.

Recommended starting direction:

- Combine A and B.
- Add founder-led details on About and trust sections.

## 4. Component Ideas

Use:

- Service path panels
- Process timeline
- Proof cards
- Quote-preparation checklist
- FAQ accordion
- WhatsApp sticky action
- Portfolio case cards
- Package comparison table

Avoid:

- Decorative card-heavy pages with no proof
- Cards inside cards
- Oversized hero with vague copy
- Carousels for critical content

## 5. Human Texture Ideas

Use carefully:

- Light paper/document texture in document-related sections
- Fine grid only where it supports diagrams or data
- Hand-annotated arrows or labels only if they look restrained
- Real screenshots with small callouts

Do not use:

- Childish doodles
- Excessive handwritten fonts
- Rough textures that reduce professionalism

## 6. First Mockup Evaluation Checklist

Before approving any mockup:

- Does the first screen say what MAFUBAM does?
- Can users choose Research or Business within seconds?
- Is WhatsApp visible without feeling spammy?
- Is there real proof above or near the fold?
- Does the design avoid generic AI-style visuals?
- Does it look credible for both students and organisations?
- Is it readable on a small Android phone?
- Are CTAs specific and useful?
- Are trust signals visible before the final CTA?
