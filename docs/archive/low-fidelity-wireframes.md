# Low-Fidelity Wireframes

These are text wireframes for planning. They describe layout and content priority before any visual design or coding.

## 1. Home

Desktop structure:

```text
[Header: Logo | Services | Portfolio | Packages | About | Contact | Request a Quote]

[Hero]
Left:
  Eyebrow: Registered technology company in Port Harcourt
  H1: Digital Solutions for Research and Business
  Short copy
  [Request a Quote] [Chat on WhatsApp]
  Trust line: MAFUBAM TECHNOLOGY LTD | CAC RC 7522288 | Port Harcourt

Right:
  Proof workbench visual:
  - document preview
  - chart preview
  - diagram preview
  - website preview

[Two Path Band]
  [Research Solutions panel]
  [Business Technology Solutions panel]

[Featured Services]
  6 compact service entries with concrete deliverables

[How It Works]
  6-step horizontal process on desktop, stacked on mobile

[Proof / Portfolio Preview]
  3 selected proof cards

[Why MAFUBAM]
  Founder-led, computer-science background, written quotes, ethical support

[FAQ]
  5-7 common questions

[Final CTA]
  Request a Quote / Chat on WhatsApp

[Footer]
```

Mobile structure:

```text

[Hero copy]

[Trust line]

```
