# Website Strategy

## 1. Primary Goal

Build trust quickly and convert visitors into qualified enquiries for MAFUBAM TECHNOLOGY LTD.

The website should answer four questions fast:

- What does MAFUBAM do?
- Is this service for me?
- Can I trust this company?
- How do I request a quote or start a conversation?

## 2. Positioning

Recommended public brand:

MAFUBAM Technology

Recommended website headline:

Digital Solutions for Research and Business

Recommended supporting copy:

MAFUBAM Technology helps researchers, professionals and growing organisations solve data, documentation, web and technology challenges through reliable, well-designed and practical digital solutions.

## 3. Audience Segments

Primary audience A: Research and Academic

- Undergraduate students
- MSc and PhD researchers
- Lecturers
- Independent researchers
- Research consultants

Primary needs:

- Data cleaning and analysis
- Charts, tables and interpretation support
- Academic formatting
- References and citations
- Technical diagrams
- Defence slides
- Programming guidance

Primary audience B: Business and Organisation

- SMEs
- Schools
- Churches
- Clinics
- Hotels
- Consultants
- NGOs
- Local service companies

Primary needs:

- Websites
- Landing pages
- Maintenance
- Digital forms
- Business documents
- Automation
- IT consultation

## 4. Conversion Strategy

Primary conversion:

- WhatsApp enquiry

Secondary conversions:

- Request a quote form
- Email enquiry
- Phone call
- Book consultation

The first version can use a WhatsApp prefilled message and email link instead of a backend form.

## 5. Trust Signals

Required trust signals:

- Registered company name: MAFUBAM TECHNOLOGY LTD
- CAC registration number: 7522288
- Port Harcourt location
- Clear working process
- Ethical research policy
- Package descriptions
- Portfolio samples
- Testimonials when available
- Privacy and confidentiality statements
- Written quotation and deposit policy

## 6. Brand Tone

Tone should be:

- Clear
- Professional
- Practical
- Direct
- Respectful
- Competent
- Human
- Specific

Avoid:

- Overpromising
- "Best in Nigeria" claims
- Academic misconduct language
- Too much technical jargon
- Informal freelancer positioning
- Generic AI-style slogans
- Claims that could belong to any digital agency

## 7. Anti-Generic Positioning

The website should not sound like a template. It should repeatedly prove that MAFUBAM is a real company serving real research and business needs.

Use specific positioning:

- Registered technology company in Port Harcourt.
- Research support plus business technology under one company.
- Data, documents, diagrams, websites and practical IT support.
- Written quote before work starts.
- Ethical research support.
- WhatsApp-first enquiry for Nigerian users.

Avoid generic positioning:

- "We transform ideas into reality."
- "We provide innovative solutions for all your needs."
- "We are your trusted partner in digital transformation."
- "We deliver cutting-edge services for businesses of all sizes."

## 8. Website Success Metrics

Track manually at first:

- WhatsApp clicks
- Quote requests
- Email enquiries
- Service page visits
- Portfolio views
- Qualified enquiries
- Conversion from enquiry to paid deposit

Later analytics:

- Google Search Console
- Lightweight analytics or Google Analytics
- WhatsApp link event tracking if a script is added
