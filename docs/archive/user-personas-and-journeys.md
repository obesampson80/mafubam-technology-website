# User Personas and Journeys

This document defines the main people the website must satisfy.

## 1. Persona A: Postgraduate Researcher

Profile:

- MSc or PhD student
- Has a research document, dataset, slides or implementation task
- Likely browsing on mobile
- May contact through WhatsApp first

Primary goals:

- Find out whether MAFUBAM can help with data analysis, formatting, diagrams or implementation.
- Understand what is ethical and allowed.
- Know what files to send.
- Get a price and delivery timeline.

Concerns:

- Confidentiality
- Deadline pressure
- Cost
- Whether the provider understands academic standards
- Fear of exposing research work publicly

Homepage needs:

- Research Solutions path visible early
- Ethical research support statement
- Proof of charts, diagrams, documents or slides
- WhatsApp CTA

Journey:

1. Lands on homepage from referral, WhatsApp, LinkedIn or search.
2. Sees "Digital Solutions for Research and Business".
3. Chooses Research Solutions.
4. Scans services and ethical support statement.
5. Checks packages or FAQ.
6. Uses WhatsApp with project details.

## 2. Persona B: Undergraduate Final-Year Student

Profile:

- Needs project formatting, diagrams, programming guidance or presentation support
- Wants simple language
- Often deadline-driven

Primary goals:

- Know if help is available quickly.
- Understand what to send.
- Avoid complicated processes.

Concerns:

- Price
- Urgent delivery
- Whether work will be accepted by department
- Whether support crosses academic rules

Homepage needs:

- Simple service names
- Clear "send files and deadline" instruction
- FAQ for pricing, timelines and revisions

Journey:

1. Opens site on phone.
2. Taps Research Solutions or Chat on WhatsApp.
3. Sends document/dataset/project details.
4. Receives quote and next steps.

## 3. Persona C: Lecturer or Research Consultant

Profile:

- More experienced research user
- May need charts, tables, report formatting, diagrams or presentation polish

Primary goals:

- Find technically competent support.
- Confirm confidentiality.
- See proof of polished work.

Concerns:

- Quality
- Professionalism
- Misrepresentation
- Reliability

Homepage needs:

- Technical proof visuals
- Clear process
- Professional tone
- Portfolio/case-study path

Journey:

1. Reviews homepage and portfolio.
2. Checks About and trust signals.
3. Sends a scoped enquiry by WhatsApp or email.

## 4. Persona D: Port Harcourt SME Owner

Profile:

- Owns a small business or service organisation
- Needs a website, redesign, content update or digital form
- Values practical communication and visible examples

Primary goals:

- Understand if MAFUBAM builds business websites.
- See what is included.
- Contact quickly.

Concerns:

- Cost
- Whether the site will work on mobile
- Whether support continues after launch
- Whether the provider is serious and reachable

Homepage needs:

- Business Technology Solutions path visible early
- Website/digital support proof
- Local credibility
- WhatsApp and phone access

Journey:

1. Lands on homepage from Google, LinkedIn, referral or WhatsApp.
2. Chooses Business Technology Solutions.
3. Checks website package/process.
4. Contacts on WhatsApp or phone.

## 5. Persona E: School, Church, Clinic or NGO Administrator

Profile:

- Needs practical digital support, website maintenance, documents, presentations or forms
- May not know the technical solution yet

Primary goals:

- Explain a problem and get guidance.
- Know whether MAFUBAM can scope the work.
- Trust the company with organisational materials.

Concerns:

- Clarity
- Formal communication
- Record keeping
- Reliability

Homepage needs:

- Clear "Discuss Your Project" path
- Process and quote explanation
- Registered company trust signals

Journey:

1. Reviews services.
2. Checks About/Contact.
3. Sends enquiry with organisational context.
4. Receives written quote.

## 6. Cross-Audience Design Implications

The website must:

- Route research and business visitors quickly.
- Avoid making research support look unethical.
- Avoid making business services look secondary.
- Use proof visuals that show technical depth without exposing client work.
- Keep WhatsApp visible but professional.
- Explain next steps after contact.
- Use language that is specific, local and human.
