# Design Direction

## 1. Design Goal

The website should feel professional, clear and trustworthy. It should look like a serious digital-services company, not a casual freelancer page and not a heavy software dashboard.

The website must also avoid the generic AI-generated look. It should feel specific to MAFUBAM, Port Harcourt, research support, business technology services and the company's real operating process.

The website must fit user taste, not only designer taste. It should feel modern to Nigerian mobile users, credible to business owners, calm to researchers, and practical to people who want to make contact quickly.

## 2. Visual Personality

Keywords:

- Clear
- Technical
- Trustworthy
- Practical
- Modern
- Organized

Avoid:

- Overdecorated tech visuals
- Excessive gradients
- Generic stock imagery
- Generic AI-generated technology illustrations
- Floating abstract shapes with no service meaning
- Dense blocks of text
- Dark, low-contrast pages
- Too many unrelated colors

## 3. Color Direction

Recommended palette:

- Primary: deep navy
- Secondary: teal or cyan
- Accent: restrained green or amber for status/highlights
- Neutrals: white, near-white, charcoal, cool grey

Rules:

- Use navy for authority, not as the only dominant color everywhere.
- Use teal/cyan for links, highlights and selected states.
- Keep backgrounds mostly light for readability.
- Check contrast for all text and buttons.
- Support dark sections or dark mode without turning the site into a neon or cyberpunk interface.
- Treat bright proof visuals as framed evidence panels on dark backgrounds.

## 4. Typography

Recommended type:

- Inter
- Source Sans 3
- Arial fallback

Rules:

- Use strong but compact headings.
- Keep body text readable on mobile.
- Avoid very thin font weights.
- Avoid letter-spacing tricks.
- Keep line lengths comfortable.

## 5. Layout Direction

Homepage:

- First viewport should immediately show the brand, headline, service promise and CTA.
- Show the two main audience paths early.
- Avoid a marketing-only hero that delays access to services.
- Show WhatsApp or quote access early, especially on mobile.
- Keep proof visible within the first scroll.

Service pages:

- Use clear service cards or sections.
- Keep deliverables scannable.
- Use "who this is for" and "what you receive" blocks.

Portfolio:

- Use consistent case-study cards.
- Favor real screenshots, diagrams and document previews.

Contact:

- Make WhatsApp, phone and email visible.
- Include Port Harcourt location and business hours.
- State what happens after the user sends a message or quote request.

## 6. Interaction Direction

Use:

- Clear button states
- Visible focus states
- Simple hover states
- Accordion FAQ if needed
- Sticky mobile WhatsApp/contact action

Avoid:

- Heavy scroll effects
- Auto-playing media
- Decorative animation that does not help users
- Popups that block reading

## 7. Image and Asset Direction

Best assets:

- Real portfolio screenshots
- Document formatting before/after samples
- Charts and dashboards
- Technical diagrams
- Website mockups
- Clean workspace or local business imagery if needed

Avoid:

- Fake 3D technology illustrations
- Random laptop stock photos
- Images that do not prove actual service quality
- AI-generated office scenes that could belong to any company

## 8. Human-First Rules

Every page should include real operational detail:

- What the client sends
- What MAFUBAM reviews
- What MAFUBAM delivers
- What is excluded
- How revisions work
- How the client requests a quote

At least one visual proof element should appear near major claims. A service claim should be supported by a sample, process step, deliverable list, screenshot, diagram, testimonial or policy.

## 9. Accessibility Rules

Design must support:

- Mobile reflow
- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus
- Adequate color contrast
- Large touch targets
- Clear form labels
- Helpful form instructions
- No reliance on color alone

## 10. User Taste Rules

The design should satisfy both research and business visitors:

- Research users should see confidentiality, ethical support, formatting quality and data-analysis proof.
- Business users should see professionalism, process, web/interface proof and practical outcomes.
- Nigerian mobile users should see fast pages, readable text, WhatsApp access and local trust signals.
- Dark mode should be comfortable and premium, not decorative or overly dramatic.
