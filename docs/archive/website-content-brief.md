## 7. Service Boundaries

MAFUBAM accepts:

- Data cleaning, analysis and result presentation
- Document formatting and reference consistency checks
- Technical diagrams and research visuals
- Presentation and defence-slide design
- Programming support, debugging guidance and tutoring
- Business websites, landing pages and redesigns
- Website maintenance and content updates
- Digital forms, lightweight automation and practical IT support

MAFUBAM refuses:

- Fabricating data
- Impersonating students
- Writing examinations
- Guaranteeing grades
- Releasing final editable files before full payment unless agreed in writing

## 8. Default Payment and Revision Rules

Use until final policy is confirmed:

- Deposit: 50%-70%, depending on project size and urgency
- Balance: due before release of final editable files or production credentials
- Revisions: 1-2 included rounds, depending on package
- Urgency fee: applies when the deadline requires accelerated work
- Scope change: new requirements after quote approval require revised pricing and timeline
- Refunds: deposits are commitment payments once work has started; refund terms depend on scope and stage
