## 2. Research Solutions

### Hero

Headline:

Research and Academic Solutions

Supporting copy:

Support for students, researchers, lecturers and consultants who need clean analysis, professional formatting, clear visuals and better technical presentation.

CTA:

Send Research Requirements

### Service Sections

Data Analysis:

For datasets that need cleaning, coding, tables, charts and explanation support. Send your dataset, research questions, analysis instructions and deadline. We review the files before confirming price and delivery time.

Document Formatting:

Professional formatting for academic and technical documents, including headings, margins, page numbering, tables, figures, references and final Word/PDF preparation.

Technical Diagrams:

Architecture diagrams, UML diagrams, flowcharts, process diagrams, research-framework visuals and redrawn figures for reports, slides and publications.

Presentation Design:

Defence slides, seminar slides, report presentations and business-style research summaries with clearer structure and visuals.

Programming Support:

Guidance, debugging and implementation support for computer-science projects and technical tasks. This is support and tutoring, not impersonation.

### Ethical Research Statement

MAFUBAM provides legitimate research support: data cleaning, analysis, formatting, diagrams, presentation design, programming guidance and tutoring. We do not fabricate data, impersonate students, write examinations or guarantee academic outcomes.
