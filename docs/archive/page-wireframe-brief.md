# Page Wireframe Brief

This is not code. It defines what each page should contain before UI design begins.

## 1. Home

Purpose:

- Explain MAFUBAM within seconds.
- Route visitors to the right service path.
- Generate WhatsApp or quote enquiries.

First screen:

- Brand: MAFUBAM Technology
- Headline: Digital Solutions for Research and Business
- Supporting copy: practical, short, location-aware
- Primary CTA: Request a Quote
- Secondary CTA: Chat on WhatsApp
- Split links:
  - Research Solutions
  - Business Technology Solutions

Hero visual:

- Use a real or sample work preview: chart, document, diagram or website screenshot.
- Do not use abstract tech art.

Sections:

- Two service paths
- Featured services
- Proof strip
- How work moves from enquiry to delivery
- Why MAFUBAM
- FAQ
- Final CTA

## 2. Research Solutions

Purpose:

- Help students, researchers and lecturers understand what support is allowed and useful.

Sections:

- Page headline
- Who this is for
- Services
- Ethical research policy
- What the client sends
- What MAFUBAM delivers
- Example outputs
- Pricing guidance
- FAQ
- CTA

Required human details:

- Actual tools used for analysis and formatting
- Accepted file formats
- Types of research support allowed
- Types of work refused

## 3. Business Technology Solutions

Purpose:

- Help organisations see MAFUBAM as a practical technology partner.

Sections:

- Page headline
- Who this is for
- Website services
- Website maintenance
- Digital forms and workflow support
- Business documents and presentations
- IT consultation
- What the client provides
- What MAFUBAM delivers
- CTA

Required human details:

- Local business types served
- Typical website deliverables
- Hosting/domain policy
- Maintenance boundaries

## 4. Portfolio

Purpose:

- Show proof of work and reduce buyer uncertainty.

Sections:

- Intro explaining confidentiality and anonymised samples
- Filter or grouping by type
- Case study cards
- Sample project cards if real work cannot be shown

Each card:

- Project type
- Problem
- Work done
- Output
- Visual sample
- Permission/confidentiality status

## 5. Packages

Purpose:

- Help visitors understand starting costs without pretending every project is identical.

Sections:

- Pricing explanation
- Research Starter
- Research Analysis
- Professional Research
- Business Website
- Website Care Plan
- Consultation/Tutoring
- What affects final quote
- Payment and revision rules
- CTA

## 6. About

Purpose:

- Make the company feel real and credible.

Sections:

- Company identity
- Founder bio
- Computer-science background
- Port Harcourt base
- How the company works
- Values
- Compliance/trust note

Required human details:

- Founder name
- Short founder story
- Real strengths
- Public photo decision

## 7. Contact

Purpose:

- Make it easy to reach MAFUBAM.

Sections:

- WhatsApp
- Phone
- Email
- Location
- Business hours
- Response expectation
- Quick enquiry prompts

## 8. Request a Quote

Purpose:

- Collect enough information to issue a written quote.

Version 1 approach:

- Static instructions plus WhatsApp/email handoff.
- Optional third-party form later.

Fields/questions:

- Name
- Organisation or school
- Contact details
- Service category
- Project description
- Deadline
- Budget range
- Existing files
- Preferred contact method

## 9. Privacy Policy

Purpose:

- Explain how customer information and files are handled.

Must cover:

- What information is collected
- Why it is collected
- How files are stored
- Confidentiality
- Retention
- Deletion requests
- Third-party tools

## 10. Terms of Service

Purpose:

- Set expectations before payment.

Must cover:

- Quotes
- Deposits
- Scope
- Revisions
- Urgent work
- Final file release
- Refunds
- Ethical research boundary
- Portfolio permission
