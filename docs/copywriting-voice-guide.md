# Copywriting Voice Guide

## 1. Voice

MAFUBAM should sound:

- Human
- Clear
- Specific
- Competent
- Local-aware
- Practical

It should not sound:

- Inflated
- Robotic
- Overly corporate
- Like a generic digital agency
- Like an academic misconduct service

## 2. Writing Principles

Use:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear headings
- Direct verbs
- Concrete service outcomes
- Plain pricing boundaries
- Real examples

Avoid:

- Long introductions
- Buzzwords
- Repeated slogans
- Generic innovation language
- Claims without proof

## 3. Approved Language Patterns

Use lines like:

- "Send the files, instructions and deadline. We review the scope before quoting."
- "For research work, we support analysis, formatting, diagrams and presentation. We do not fabricate data or impersonate students."
- "For websites, content, domain and hosting are confirmed before development starts."
- "Every project begins with a written scope, price and delivery expectation."
- "Based in Port Harcourt, serving clients locally and remotely."

## 4. Banned or Rewrite Language

Avoid lines like:

- "We empower businesses with innovative digital solutions."
- "Your trusted partner for digital transformation."
- "We bring your dreams to life."
- "Cutting-edge solutions for the modern world."
- "Unlock unlimited possibilities."
- "Seamless solutions tailored to your needs."

Rewrite example:

Bad:

"We provide cutting-edge digital solutions that transform your vision into reality."

Better:

"We build practical websites, documents, diagrams and data outputs that help clients present work clearly and handle business tasks better."

## 5. CTA Language

Primary CTAs:

- Request a Quote
- Chat on WhatsApp
- Send Project Details
- Discuss Your Project

Secondary CTAs:

- View Research Services
- Explore Business Solutions
- See Packages
- View Portfolio

Avoid:

- Get Started Today
- Unlock Growth
- Transform Now
- Learn More

## 6. Service Copy Formula

Each service should follow this formula:

- What it solves
- Who it is for
- What the client sends
- What MAFUBAM delivers
- What affects price or timeline
- CTA

Example:

Data analysis:

"For students, researchers and consultants who already have data and need clean tables, charts and interpretation support. Send the dataset, research questions, required analysis method and deadline. We review the files, confirm the scope, then deliver agreed tables, charts and explanation notes."

## 7. Ethical Research Copy

Use:

"MAFUBAM provides legitimate research support: data cleaning, analysis, formatting, diagrams, presentation design, programming guidance and tutoring. We do not fabricate data, impersonate students, write examinations or guarantee academic outcomes."

Avoid:

- "We write your thesis for you."
- "Guaranteed A grade."
- "Submit-ready project writing."
- "Complete Chapter 1-5 service."

## 8. Local Trust Copy

Use Port Harcourt naturally:

- "Based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State."
- "Available for remote work across Nigeria."
- "Local meetings or on-site support may be arranged when necessary."

Avoid forcing local keywords into every paragraph.
