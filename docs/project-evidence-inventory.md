- Do not expose student names, client names, school names, matric numbers, phone numbers or emails.
- Do not expose raw datasets unless converted to dummy/sample data.
- Do not publish Turnitin reports as proof in a way that identifies a client or academic submission.
- Do not show full thesis/project documents.
- Use cropped, blurred or recreated excerpts.
- Label confidential work as anonymised.
- Label sample/demo work honestly.
- Get permission before showing identifiable client work.
