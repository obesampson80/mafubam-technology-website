# LinkedIn Setup Guide

This guide explains how to create, reopen and improve the MAFUBAM Technology LinkedIn presence.

## Recommended Structure

Use two LinkedIn presence points:

- Obe Sampson personal profile: founder credibility, professional background and trust.
- MAFUBAM Technology Company Page: official business page for services, website link, posts and quote enquiries.

The website remains the main proof and conversion point. LinkedIn should send people back to the website or WhatsApp quote flow.

## Create The Company Page

Use LinkedIn on desktop if possible.

1. Log in to your personal LinkedIn account.
2. Click `For Business` in the top-right menu.
3. Scroll down and choose `Create a Company Page`.
4. Select `Company`.
5. Fill the page details:

```text
Name: MAFUBAM Technology
Public URL: mafubam-technology
Website: https://obesampson80.github.io/mafubam-technology-website/
Industry: IT Services and IT Consulting
Company size: 1 employee or 2-10 employees
Company type: Privately Held
Location: Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria
Tagline: Research, web and practical technology support for students, professionals and growing businesses.
```

6. Tick the confirmation box showing you are authorized to create the page.
7. Click `Create page`.

## How To Reopen The Page On A Fresh Start

LinkedIn Company Pages do not have separate login details. You always log in through your personal LinkedIn profile that has admin access.

### Desktop

1. Sign in to your personal LinkedIn account.
2. Go to the LinkedIn home page.
3. Look at the left side of the home page for `My pages`.
4. Click `MAFUBAM Technology`.
5. If you do not see it, click `See all`.

Alternative desktop path:

1. Sign in to LinkedIn.
2. Click the `Me` dropdown at the top of LinkedIn.
3. Look for `Manage`.
4. Select `MAFUBAM Technology`.

### Mobile App

1. Open the LinkedIn app.
2. Sign in with your personal LinkedIn profile.
3. Tap your profile picture in the upper-left corner.
4. Look for `Manage pages`.
5. Tap `MAFUBAM Technology`.
6. If it is not visible, tap `See all`.

If the page does not show anywhere, confirm you are signed in to the same personal LinkedIn profile that created the Company Page.

## Add Logo And Banner

Use:

- Logo: MAFUBAM Technology brand mark from the website.
- Banner: a clean LinkedIn banner that matches the website style.

Do not use a random stock image or generic AI-looking banner. The banner should feel like the website: professional, proof-led, practical and technology-focused.

## Notes

- LinkedIn Pages are managed through personal profiles with admin access.
- The person who creates the Company Page is usually the page admin.
- If the page is not visible, check that you are using the same LinkedIn account that created it.
- Do not share LinkedIn login details with anyone. Add admins properly if needed.
